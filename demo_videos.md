# Demo Videos for VVC Video Compression Dataset

Welcome to the **VVC Video Compression Dataset** demo section. Here are some sample videos demonstrating various compression techniques and background settings from the dataset. Click on the videos to get a better understanding of the dataset.

## 1. Dynamic Background (Texture, Simple Background, No Compression, Camera Movement)
[![Dynamic Background](dynamic_longdress.mp4)

- **Description**: This video demonstrates a dynamic background where we have a texture background and a simple background with no compression applied. The camera movement adds complexity to the scene.

## 2. Different Background (Grey and Black Background, No Compression)
[![Different Background](https://img.youtube.com/vi/longdress_vox10_black_db/0.jpg)](https://github.com/FHossain5/VVC-Video-Compression-Dataset/blob/main/longdress_vox10_black_db.mp4)

- **Description**: This video shows how different background setups, including grey and black backgrounds, look with no compression.

## 3. Geometry Compression (R1 to R5 Levels)
[![Geometry Compression](https://img.youtube.com/vi/R1_longdress_black_10_vox10_gc/0.jpg)](https://github.com/FHossain5/VVC-Video-Compression-Dataset/blob/main/R1_longdress_black_10_vox10_gc.mp4)

- **Description**: This video demonstrates geometry compression applied at different levels (R1 to R5). Compression affects the visual fidelity and size.

## 4. Texture Compression (R1 to R5 Levels)
[![Texture Compression](https://img.youtube.com/vi/R1_longdress_black_10_vox10_tc/0.jpg)](https://github.com/FHossain5/VVC-Video-Compression-Dataset/blob/main/R1_longdress_black_10_vox10_tc.mp4)

- **Description**: Here we demonstrate texture compression applied at varying levels (R1 to R5). Watch the differences in visual quality as compression increases.

## 5. Geometry and Texture Compression (R1 to R5 Levels)
[![Geometry and Texture Compression](https://img.youtube.com/vi/R1_longdress_black_10_vox10_gtc/0.jpg)](https://github.com/FHossain5/VVC-Video-Compression-Dataset/blob/main/R1_longdress_black_10_vox10_gtc.mp4)

- **Description**: This video demonstrates both geometry and texture compression applied together, showing the results of R1 to R5 levels.
